What is routing?

Routing is the process of determining what content to display based on the URL the user
navigates to.  SPA's update the url dependendent content without reloading the entire
page.

Routing is mapping URL's to the content or components that should render for them.

/ usually means home component
/about can be the about component
/products can be the products component etc

The router tells the app what to render based on which components belong to the chosen URL.



How should you add links to different routes on your pages?
Links should not be added with <a href=''> but rather like this: <Link to='/'>Home</Link>
